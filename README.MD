## POWERBI | Auto Insurance and Claims Analytics Dashboard

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze automobile insurance policies, customer characteristics, vehicle information, and insurance claim behavior. The dashboard is designed to help decision-makers identify customer segments, vehicle categories, and geographic areas associated with higher insurance claims while providing a comprehensive overview of the insurance portfolio.
## Executive Summary
Insurance companies rely on data to understand customer risk, evaluate claim patterns, and support pricing and underwriting decisions. This dashboard transforms raw insurance policy data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and dashboard reporting.

The report is organized into multiple analytical sections that progressively explore the insurance portfolio, customer demographics, household characteristics, vehicle information, and overall risk exposure.
